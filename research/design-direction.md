# AY portfolio design

## Reference study, 30 October 2026

1. Dennis Snellenberg: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/dennis-snellenberg
   Live site inspected. Personal photography anchors the opening; small navigation and enormous type create a clear hierarchy. AY adopts the personal presence and restrained navigation.
2. Artem Shcherban: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/artem-shcherban-portfolio
   Awwwards listing and submitted screenshot inspected; the live opening remained behind its introductory interaction. Expressive serif typography and generous empty space are the relevant references. AY adopts the typographic contrast, without duplicating the visual identity.
3. Melanie Daveid: https://www.awwwards.com/sites/melanie-daveid
   Current live site inspected. Focused text, minimal navigation and strong photographic surfaces keep the work legible. AY uses restrained image treatment and simple page compositions.

## Direction

- Stone #f4f2ef; plum #382b33; soft stone #e9e4df; muted ink #71676b; dusty plum #896c7a.
- Bodoni Moda for the editorial voice; DM Sans for reading and navigation.
- Home: a large title with a central portrait and compact side introduction.
- About: portrait and personal story; Services: capability disclosures; Skills & Tools: skills index and grouped tools; Case Studies: asymmetric photographic index and separate detail pages; Credentials: testimonial and certificate collections; Contact: personal invitation and email draft composer.
- One main type gesture per page; quiet controls; regular browser scrolling; responsive layout; reduced motion.
- The reference choices guide composition rather than reproducing another person's site.

The client asked to assume an experienced EA and focus on design. The email address is an authorised dummy. The portrait is a temporary stock image, visibly credited.
